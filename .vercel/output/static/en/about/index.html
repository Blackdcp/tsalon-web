01 / WHO WE ARE

From an iOS developer group to an open community for what comes next

T Salon was founded in March 2016 by a group of iOS developers. Starting with the Apple developer ecosystem, the community gradually expanded into AI technology and business, embodied intelligence and wider engineering practice.

We have organised gatherings in Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Hangzhou, while livestreams, interviews and online roundtables carry conversations across cities. The technologies change; our question stays the same: what problems are practitioners facing, and what choices are they making?
